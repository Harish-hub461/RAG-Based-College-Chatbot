OFFICIAL ADMISSIONS AND FEE STRUCTURE BULLETIN (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027)
================================================================================

1. ADMISSION ELIGIBILITY & CRITERIA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Candidates seeking admission to the Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) program must meet the following eligibility requirements:
- Educational Qualification: Passed 10+2 (Higher Secondary Examination) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) as compulsory subjects.
- Minimum Marks: Aggregate minimum of 60% marks in PCM (55% for SC/ST candidates).
- Entrance Examination: Valid score in JEE Main or State Level Engineering Entrance Test (EET-2026).
- Merit Selection: Admissions are conducted through centralized counseling based on rank lists.

2. CSE DEGREE FEE STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The annual tuition and fee details for B.Tech CSE (4-Year Degree) are as follows:

A. Tuition Fee:
   - Semester 1 Fee: $2,500 USD (or local equivalent INR 1,80,000)
   - Semester 2 Fee: $2,500 USD (or local equivalent INR 1,80,000)
   - Total Annual Tuition Fee: $5,000 USD per year.

B. Additional Institutional Charges:
   - Admission Processing Fee (One-time, non-refundable): $150 USD
   - Library & Laboratory Security Deposit (Refundable upon graduation): $200 USD
   - Student Development & Cultural Activity Fee: $100 USD per year
   - Semester Examination Fee: $80 USD per semester

C. Payment Deadlines:
   - Autumn Semester Fee Payment: Due on or before July 15, 2026.
   - Spring Semester Fee Payment: Due on or before December 20, 2026.
   - Late Fee Fine: A fine of $10 per day applies for delayed payments up to 15 days.

3. EXAMINATION RULES AND GRADING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Attendance Requirement: Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in every registered course to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
- Grading System: 10-point Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) system.
- Passing Criteria: Minimum grade 'D' (40% marks) in each individual course.
