1. SCHOLARSHIP OPPORTUNITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The institute provides financial assistance programs to support deserving and underprivileged students:

A. Merit Scholarship:
   - Awarded to top 5% of students in each department achieving a CGPA of 9.2 or higher.
   - Benefit: 50% waiver on annual tuition fees.

B. Need-Cum-Merit Scholarship:
   - Available to students with annual family income under $6,000 USD (INR 5,00,000) and maintaining CGPA above 7.5.
   - Benefit: 30% waiver on annual tuition fees plus free hostel mess subsidy.

C. Sports & Cultural Excellence Grant:
   - Awarded to national-level athletes or performers.
   - Benefit: $500 USD annual stipend plus customized academic exam schedules.

2. CAMPUS PLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY & GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Placement Registration: Final year students must register with the Placement Cell during the 6th semester.
- Academic Cutoff: Minimum CGPA of 6.5 with no active backlogs/cleared Arrears at the time of recruitment drives.
- Average Package (CSE): $14,000 USD per annum (INR 12 LPA).
- Highest Package Offered (2025-2026): $48,000 USD per annum (INR 42 LPA) by global tech conglomerates.
- Recruiting Companies: Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Infosys, TCS, Accenture, and Deloitte.
