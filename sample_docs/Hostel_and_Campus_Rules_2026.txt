================================================================================
EXCELLENCE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY
HOSTEL MANAGEMENT & CAMPUS RESIDENCE POLICIES (2026-2027)
================================================================================

1. HOSTEL ACCOMMODATION & FEES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The campus offers air-conditioned and non-air-conditioned hostel accommodations for male and female students:

- Single Occupancy (A/C Room): $1,200 USD per semester (includes high-speed Wi-Fi & laundry).
- Double Occupancy (Non-A/C Room): $750 USD per semester per student.
- Quad Sharing Room (Non-A/C Room): $500 USD per semester per student.
- Mess & Dining Charges: $600 USD per semester (provides 4 meals daily: Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, and Dinner).

2. HOSTEL TIMINGS AND CURFEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Main Gate Entry Cutoff: All hostel residents must return to the hostel premises by 9:30 PM on weekdays (Monday to Friday).
- Weekend Timings: On Saturdays and Sundays, entry is permitted until 10:30 PM.
- Night Pass Request: Students intending to stay out overnight must submit an online Night Pass Application 24 hours in advance, approved by their Warden and verified via guardian SMS notification.

3. LIBRARY & STUDY ROOM FACILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Central Library Hours: Open 24/7 during mid-term and end-semester examination periods. Standard working hours are 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM daily.
- Digital Library: Offers access to IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, and Springer journals for registered students.
